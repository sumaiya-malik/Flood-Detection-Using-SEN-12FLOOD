I have used the SEN12-FLOOD dataset to detect flooded areas from co-registered optical (Sentinel-2) and Synthetic Aperture Radar (Sentinel-1) image time series. Distributed through IEEE DataPort, the dataset comprises 336 time series capturing regions affected by significant flooding events during the 2019 winter season, with acquisitions spanning December 2018 to May 2019. The imagery covers flood-prone areas across West Africa, South-East Africa, the Middle East, and Australia. Each time series is organised into multiple tiles of 512×512 pixels, where every tile represents a cropped subregion of an individual acquisition.

A summary of the dataset's principal characteristics is given below:

| Number of sequences | Tile dimension | Locations | 
| ------------- |------------- | -----|
| 336 | 512x512 |West Africa, South-East Africa, Middle East, Australia |

The dataset is available on the [IEEE dataport](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/sen12-flood-sar-and-multispectral-dataset-flood-detection).
